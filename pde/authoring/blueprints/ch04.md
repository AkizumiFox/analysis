# PDE.4: Sobolev Spaces

Directory: `src/ch76-sobolev-spaces/`. State: not started.

**Part VI, Functional Analysis and Distributions.** The Dirichlet problem forces the move from function spaces to abstract ones: the Part opens with the general topology it needs (topological spaces, products and compactness; separation, Urysohn–Tietze and locally compact spaces), settles what was moved out of measure theory (Lᵖ duality, Riesz–Markov, weak and weak* topologies, with Tychonoff just before Banach–Alaoglu), builds Hahn–Banach, Baire, Hilbert and compact-operator theory, then distributions, 𝒮′, interpolation and Sobolev spaces, and ends with the Lax–Milgram solution of the Dirichlet problem and the Dirichlet-Laplacian eigenbasis.

*Traces, Poincaré, H¹₀ and compactness are the next chapter. Brezis's one-dimensional chapter is the gentle entry; Evans supplies the n-dimensional statements.*

5. The embedding Hˢ(ℝⁿ) ⊂ C_b for s > n/2; failure at s = n/2 in ℝ² via log log
6. Gagliardo–Nirenberg–Sobolev and Morrey inequalities; extension operators (statements and proof ideas)

## Must prove

- W^{k,p}(Ω) is a Banach space
- W^{1,p}(I) functions have absolutely continuous representatives with u(x)−u(y)=∫u′
- interior approximation by smooth functions
- Hᵏ(ℝⁿ) = W^{k,2}(ℝⁿ) with equivalent norms
- Hˢ(ℝⁿ) ↪ C_b(ℝⁿ) for s > n/2

## Key examples and counterexamples

- |x| ∈ W^{1,∞}(−1,1), Heaviside ∉ W^{1,1}
- H¹(I) ⊂ C(Ī) in dimension 1
- log log(1 + 1/|x|) ∈ H¹ near 0 in ℝ² but unbounded
- |x|^{−α} ∈ W^{1,p}(B) iff α < (n−p)/p

## Exit checks (C-exercises)

- Show H¹ embeds in C in dimension 1 but not in dimension 2
- Prove Hˢ(ℝⁿ) ⊂ C_b for s > n/2 by Fourier inversion
