## Must prove

- inversion and Plancherel on ℤ(N)
- FFT recursion and operation count
- orthogonality relations for characters
- the characters of a finite abelian group form an orthonormal basis (so |Ĝ| = |G|)

## Key examples and counterexamples

- characters of ℤ(N)
- characters of ℤ*(8) and of ℤ(2)×ℤ(2)
- ℤ*(q) for q prime is cyclic (stated or exercise)
