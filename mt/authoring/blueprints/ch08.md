# MT.8: Modes of Convergence, Uniform Integrability and Weak Lᵖ

Directory: `src/ch50-modes-of-convergence/`. State: not started.

**Part IV, Measure and Integration.** Starting from Part III's list of what the Riemann integral lacks, build Lebesgue measure and the Lebesgue integral on ℝᵈ, then abstract measures, products (Fubini–Tonelli and change of variables in full), surfaces and the divergence theorem, Lᵖ, modes of convergence, differentiation, and signed measures up to Radon–Nikodym, ending with probability on measure spaces (the laws of large numbers and the central limit theorem). Lusin and the density of C_c use only the metric Urysohn and Tietze theorems of Part II.

*Uniform integrability and the Vitali convergence theorem are original writing (no assigned source treats them beyond exercises). Weak L¹ is set up here because lebesgue-differentiation's maximal inequality is a weak-(1,1) statement.*

**Page budget:** about 36 source-book pages.

**Prerequisites (from the DAG):** MT.1 (Lebesgue Measure on ℝᵈ and Measurable Functions), MT.2 (The Lebesgue Integral and the Convergence Theorems).

## Planned sections

1. The modes: a.e., uniform, almost uniform, in measure, in Lᵖ; Egorov revisited as 'a.e. ⇒ almost uniform on finite measure'
2. The four sequences (typewriter, n·1_(0,1/n), 1_[n,n+1], n^{−1/p}·1_[0,n]) and the full implication diagram; Riesz: in measure ⇒ a.e.-convergent subsequence
3. Uniform integrability; the Vitali convergence theorem (finite measure, then with tightness); de la Vallée-Poussin criterion
4. Choosing a tool: MCT, DCT, Fatou or Vitali (the limit-exchange checklist, final form)
5. The distribution function; Chebyshev–Markov; the layer-cake formula ∫|f|ᵖ = p∫₀^∞ λ^{p−1} μ(|f|>λ) dλ
6. Weak Lᵖ: the quasi-norm, Lᵖ ⊂ weak Lᵖ, |x|^{−d/p} ∈ weak Lᵖ \ Lᵖ

## Must prove

- convergence in measure ⇒ a.e.-convergent subsequence
- Lᵖ convergence ⇒ convergence in measure
- a.e. ⇒ in measure on finite measure spaces
- Vitali convergence theorem: fₙ → f a.e., (fₙ) uniformly integrable, μ(X) < ∞ ⇒ fₙ → f in L¹
- on finite measure, fₙ → f in L¹ ⇔ UI + in measure
- layer-cake formula (by Tonelli)
- Chebyshev inequality; ‖f‖_{p,∞} ≤ ‖f‖_p

## Key examples and counterexamples

- typewriter sequence
- n·1_(0,1/n)
- 1_[n,n+1]
- n^{−1/p}·1_[0,n]
- |x|^{−d/p} in weak Lᵖ but not Lᵖ
- 1/x on (0,1) in weak L¹
