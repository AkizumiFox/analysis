# Chapter 44: Abstract Measure Spaces and Carathéodory's Construction

Directory: `src/ch44-abstract-measure/`. State: not started.

**Part IV, Measure and Integration.** Starting from Part III's list of what the Riemann integral lacks, build Lebesgue measure and the Lebesgue integral on ℝᵈ, then abstract measures, products (Fubini–Tonelli and change of variables in full), Lᵖ, modes of convergence, differentiation, and signed measures up to Radon–Nikodym, preceded by the Urysohn and Tietze theorems that Lusin and the density of C_c need.

*Spiral return (decisions.md 6): the ℝᵈ construction of lebesgue-measure is recalled and reframed. π-λ is written originally (FOL and MIRA use the monotone class theorem; include both, π-λ as the main tool).*

**Page budget:** about 48 source-book pages.

**Prerequisites (from the DAG):** Chapter 42 (Lebesgue Measure on ℝᵈ and Measurable Functions), Chapter 43 (The Lebesgue Integral and the Convergence Theorems).

## Planned sections

1. σ-algebras, generated σ-algebras, Borel σ-algebras; measurable maps between measurable spaces
2. Measures, continuity properties, null sets, completion
3. Outer measures and Carathéodory's theorem; Lebesgue measure recovered
4. Metric outer measures: Borel sets are Carathéodory-measurable
5. Premeasures on algebras and the Carathéodory–Hahn extension; the π-λ (Dynkin) theorem and uniqueness of extensions; monotone class theorem
6. Lebesgue–Stieltjes measures on ℝ: increasing right-continuous F ↔ locally finite Borel μ_F; regularity
7. Integration on an abstract measure space: the convergence theorems and L¹(μ) transferred; counting measure and series

## Must prove

- Carathéodory's theorem
- Borel sets are measurable for a metric outer measure
- extension of a premeasure; uniqueness for σ-finite premeasures via π-λ
- π-λ theorem
- correspondence F ↔ μ_F for Lebesgue–Stieltjes measures
- outer and inner regularity of Lebesgue–Stieltjes measures
- MCT, Fatou, DCT on an abstract measure space (by pointing to the transferred proofs)

## Key examples and counterexamples

- counting measure, Dirac measure
- μ_F for the Cantor function F (singular continuous)
- a non-σ-finite measure where uniqueness fails
- completion: Lebesgue σ-algebra is the completion of the Borel σ-algebra

## Exit checks (C-exercises)

- Prove Carathéodory's theorem
- Prove uniqueness of measures agreeing on a π-system via the π-λ theorem
