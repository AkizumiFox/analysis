## Key examples and counterexamples

- log|z| harmonic on ℂ∖{0} with no harmonic conjugate
- the Poisson kernel as an approximate identity
- log|f| is subharmonic for holomorphic f
- the punctured disc: the Dirichlet problem with data 0 on |z|=1 and 1 at 0 has no solution (irregular point)
