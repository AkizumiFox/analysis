# Chapter 29: The Riemann Integral on ℝⁿ

Directory: `src/ch29-riemann-integral-rn/`. State: not started.

**Part II, Metric Spaces and Several Variables.** Lifts convergence, completeness, compactness and continuity from ℝ to metric spaces and C(K), forced by concrete problems (ODE existence and uniqueness, polynomial approximation, typical continuous functions), then does calculus in ℝⁿ (inverse and implicit functions, the Riemann integral on ℝⁿ) and ends by abstracting open sets into topological spaces.

*One-week module (decisions.md 10): the general C¹ change of variables and Fubini–Tonelli are proved in Part IV; here state the C¹ formula only as a forward reference and do not use it in proofs. Green's theorem (rectangle and disc) is a named theorem node (decisions.md 12; label e.g. thm-green) needed by harmonic functions in Part V and by PDE; prove it for regions that are both type I and type II using Fubini + FTC, no forms. Polar coordinates (hence ∫e^{−x²}) must wait for Part IV or use a one-variable argument.*

## Planned sections

1. Rectangles, partitions and Darboux sums in ℝⁿ; integrability criterion; continuous functions on rectangles are integrable
2. Sets of content zero and measure zero; functions whose discontinuities have content zero are integrable (Lebesgue's criterion stated, spiral from Part I)
3. Jordan content: Jordan-measurable sets (boundary of content zero), the integral over a bounded Jordan set, additivity; sets without content
4. Fubini for continuous functions on rectangles and on regions between graphs; Cavalieri; iterated integrals can exist and disagree without integrability
5. Differentiation under the integral sign (Leibniz rule)
6. Linear change of variables: vol(T(A)) = |det T|·vol(A) and ∫f(Tx)|det T| dx = ∫f, proved via elementary matrices and Fubini; volume of a parallelepiped; statement of the C¹ formula with proof deferred to Part IV
7. Curves and line integrals; Green's theorem on a rectangle and on a disc

## Must prove

- continuous functions on a closed rectangle are integrable
- discontinuity set of content zero ⇒ integrable
- Jordan-measurable sets form an algebra; integral over a Jordan set is well defined
- Fubini for continuous functions on rectangles and on type-I regions
- differentiation under the integral sign for continuous ∂f/∂t
- vol(T(A)) = |det T| vol(A) for linear T and Jordan A
- linear change-of-variables formula
- Green's theorem on a rectangle and on a disc

## Key examples and counterexamples

- ℚ² ∩ [0,1]²: bounded, no content
- a compact set without content (fat Cantor square, GO.11)
- a function whose iterated integrals both exist and differ (GO.9)
- f(x,y) = (x²−y²)/(x²+y²)² on (0,1]²
- area of an ellipse by a linear change of variables
- Green's theorem computing the area of a disc via ∮x dy

## Exit checks (C-exercises)

- prove vol(T(A)) = |det T| vol(A) for an elementary matrix, then in general
- verify Green's theorem on the unit disc for P = −y, Q = x and prove it on a rectangle
