# Chapter 36: Mean-Square and Pointwise Convergence

Directory: `src/ch36-mean-square-pointwise/`. State: not started.

**Part III, Fourier Analysis in the Riemann Setting.** Fourier series and the Fourier transform on 𝒮(ℝᵈ), built entirely on the Riemann integral: summability, mean-square and pointwise convergence, applications, and (optionally) finite abelian groups and Dirichlet's theorem. The Part ends by listing what the Riemann framework cannot do, and that list motivates Part IV.

*The divergence example is done by explicit construction here; Part VI redoes it by the uniform boundedness principle (curriculum S4 (d)). Record the first entry of the 'what we need' list: ℛ(𝕋) is not complete, so Parseval gives an isometry into ℓ² that is not onto. Linear-algebra gate (route R8): inner products, orthogonal projection, the spectral theorem and SVD from the companion book are done before this chapter; the Part III opener states this.*
