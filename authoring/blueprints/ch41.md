4. Urysohn's lemma for normal spaces (dyadic-rational construction)

## Must prove

- metric spaces and compact Hausdorff spaces are normal
- Urysohn's lemma
- Tietze extension theorem (real-valued, closed subset of a normal space)

- LCH Urysohn lemma
- partitions of unity on a compact subset of an LCH space

## Key examples and counterexamples

- distance-function Urysohn functions in ℝᵈ
- Tietze fails for non-closed subsets (sin(1/x) on (0,1])
- ℕ and ℝⁿ one-point compactifications
- an infinite-dimensional normed space is not locally compact (pointer to Part II's ℓ²)
