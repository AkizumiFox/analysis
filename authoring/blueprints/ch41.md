1. Elementary number theory: the fundamental theorem of arithmetic, infinitude of primes

## Must prove

- Euler product for ζ(s)
- Σ 1/p diverges
- L(1,χ) ≠ 0 for χ ≠ χ₀
- Dirichlet's theorem

## Key examples and counterexamples

- primes ≡ 1 and ≡ 3 mod 4 via χ mod 4
- the divisor sum Σ_{n≤N} d(n) = N log N + O(N)
