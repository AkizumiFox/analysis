# Chapter 65: Radon Measures and the Dual of C₀(X)

Directory: `src/ch65-riesz-markov/`. State: not started.

**Part VI, Functional Analysis and Distributions.** The Dirichlet problem forces the move from function spaces to abstract ones: the Part opens with the general topology it needs (topological spaces, products and compactness; separation, Urysohn–Tietze and locally compact spaces), settles what was moved out of measure theory (Lᵖ duality, Riesz–Markov, weak and weak* topologies, with Tychonoff just before Banach–Alaoglu), builds Hahn–Banach, Baire, Hilbert and compact-operator theory, then distributions, 𝒮′, interpolation and Sobolev spaces, and ends with the Lax–Milgram solution of the Dirichlet problem and the Dirichlet-Laplacian eigenbasis.

*Riesz–Markov is placed in Part VI per decisions.md 9. It uses Part IV's measure construction and the LCH Urysohn lemma and partitions of unity of separation-urysohn-lch, not Hahn–Banach, so it can precede hahn-banach; outline-level proof is allowed by the curriculum but the C(K), K compact metric case should be complete.*

## Planned sections

1. Why functionals on continuous functions are measures: Riemann–Stieltjes on [a,b] as the prototype
2. Positive linear functionals on C_c(X) are automatically bounded on each C(K)
3. Construction of the measure from open sets; Urysohn partitions of unity; the Riesz–Markov theorem
4. Regularity of Radon measures; density of C_c in Lᵖ(μ) revisited
5. Complex Radon measures, total variation norm, the Banach space M(X)
6. The dual of C₀(X) is M(X); decomposition of a real functional into positive parts
7. First look at weak* convergence: δ_{x_n} → δ_x, Riemann sums as measures converging to Lebesgue

## Must prove

- Riesz–Markov for positive linear functionals on C_c(X), X locally compact Hausdorff
- outer and inner regularity of the constructed measure
- a bounded real functional on C₀(X) is a difference of positive ones
- C₀(X)* ≅ M(X) isometrically

## Key examples and counterexamples

- δ_x as a functional on C(K)
- Lebesgue measure recovered from the Riemann integral on C_c(ℝ)
- a positive functional on C_c(ℝ) not extendable boundedly to C₀(ℝ)
