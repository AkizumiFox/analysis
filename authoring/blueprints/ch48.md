## Must prove

- Green's theorem on a rectangle and on a disc
- surface measure on a C¹ hypersurface is well defined (independent of the local graphs and of the partition of unity)
- existence of smooth partitions of unity subordinate to a finite open cover of a compact subset of ℝⁿ
- the divergence theorem for C¹ vector fields on a bounded C¹ domain
- Green's first and second identities
- the mean value property of harmonic functions on balls in ℝⁿ, and the maximum principle
- σ(S^{d−1}) = d·|B₁| = 2π^{d/2}/Γ(d/2)

## Key examples and counterexamples

- the area of a disc by ∮x dy
- the unit sphere as two graphs; the area of S² is 4π
- x/|x|ᵈ is divergence-free away from 0, yet its flux through every sphere about 0 is σ(S^{d−1})
- a domain with an outward cusp, where the C¹ hypothesis fails
- the fundamental solution Φ(x) = −(1/2π) log|x| in ℝ² and c_d|x|^{2−d} for d ≥ 3
