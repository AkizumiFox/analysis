# Chapter 63: Baire Category and the Fundamental Theorems for Operators

Directory: `src/ch63-baire-category-operators/`. State: not started.

**Part VI, Functional Analysis and Distributions.** The Dirichlet problem forces the move from function spaces to abstract ones: the Part first settles what was moved out of measure theory (Lᵖ duality, Riesz–Markov, weak and weak* topologies, with Tychonoff just before Banach–Alaoglu), builds Hahn–Banach, Baire, Hilbert and compact-operator theory, then distributions, 𝒮′ and Sobolev spaces, and ends with the Lax–Milgram solution of the Dirichlet problem and the Dirichlet-Laplacian eigenbasis.

*Second appearance of Baire (first in Part II with nowhere-differentiable functions); the text explicitly recalls it. Placed before weak topologies because weakly convergent sequences are bounded only by the UBP.*

## Key examples and counterexamples

- the Dirichlet partial-sum functionals with norms ~ log N
- two comparable complete norms are equivalent
- an everywhere-defined symmetric operator on a Hilbert space is bounded (Hellinger–Toeplitz)
- UBP fails on an incomplete space (c₀₀ with sup norm)
