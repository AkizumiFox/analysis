## Must prove

- a complete ordered field is Archimedean and ℚ is dense in it
- any two complete ordered fields are isomorphic, by a unique isomorphism
- ℝ(x) with the 'eventually positive' order is an ordered field that is not Archimedean, so not complete
- 0.999… = 1 once a decimal is defined as the sup of its truncations

## Key examples and counterexamples

- ℝ(x): x is an infinitely large element
- ℚ(√2) is Archimedean but not complete
