Phragmén–Lindelöf on a strip is cited by interpolation (Part VI) for the three-lines lemma.*

## Key examples and counterexamples

- z⁵+15z+1 has all five zeros in |z|<2, exactly one in |z|<3/2
- a locally uniform limit of injective holomorphic maps is injective or constant
- exp(exp z) on the strip |Im z| < π/2: bounded on the boundary, unbounded inside, so the growth hypothesis in Phragmén–Lindelöf is sharp
- annuli: Cauchy fails for the circle |z|=1 in ℂ∖{0}
