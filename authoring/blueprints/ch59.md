# Chapter 59: Conformal Maps, Normal Families and the Riemann Mapping Theorem

Directory: `src/ch59-conformal-riemann-mapping/`. State: not started.

**Part V, Complex Analysis.** Complex differentiability turns out to be so rigid that one integral formula controls everything; the Part runs from Goursat to the Riemann mapping theorem and then through the qualifying-exam second layer (harmonic and subharmonic functions, normal families, products, continuation, Picard), with normal families as the complex spiral node of Arzelà–Ascoli.

*Normal families enter here with explicit recall of Arzelà–Ascoli from Part II (spiral: compact in metric/C(K)). Schwarz–Christoffel and elliptic integrals (SS2 §8.4) are cut.*

## Planned sections

1. Möbius transformations: circles to circles, cross ratio, symmetry principle
2. A gallery of conformal maps: disc ↔ half-plane, sectors, strips, the slit plane; the Dirichlet problem in a strip by transplantation
3. Schwarz's lemma, Schwarz–Pick, and the automorphism groups of the disc and the half-plane
4. The space H(Ω) with the metric of locally uniform convergence; completeness
5. Normal families and Montel's theorem (the complex spiral node of Arzelà–Ascoli)
6. The Riemann mapping theorem: the extremal problem, compactness, Hurwitz for injectivity
7. Consequences and limits: ℂ is not conformally equivalent to the disc; annuli with different moduli; boundary behaviour (statement)

## Must prove

- Schwarz's lemma
- Aut(𝔻) consists of the maps e^{iθ}(z−a)/(1−āz)
- H(Ω) is complete under the metric of locally uniform convergence
- Montel's theorem
- the Riemann mapping theorem

## Key examples and counterexamples

- the Cayley map z ↦ (z−i)/(z+i)
- ℂ and 𝔻 are homeomorphic but not conformally equivalent (Liouville)
- {zⁿ} on 𝔻 is normal, on a neighbourhood of a boundary point is not
- annuli r<|z|<1 and s<|z|<1 are not equivalent for r≠s (statement; proof as exercise)
