# Chapter 19: Constructing ℝ by Dedekind Cuts

Directory: `src/ch19-reals-dedekind-cuts/`. State: not started.

**Part I, Analysis on the Real Line.** Starting from the least upper bound axiom alone, Part I builds ε-N and ε-δ fluency on ℝ through sequences, series, the topology of the line, continuity, the derivative, the Riemann–Darboux integral, uniform convergence and power series, because each classical theorem of calculus (IVT, EVT, MVT, FTC, term-by-term differentiation) fails or is unprovable without completeness; it closes by proving that the forms of completeness are equivalent.

## Must prove

- the union of a nonempty bounded-above set of cuts is a cut and is its least upper bound
- the sum of two cuts is a cut, and α + (−α) = 0*
- q ↦ q* is an order-preserving injective ring homomorphism ℚ → cuts
- the cut of √2 squares to 2*

## Key examples and counterexamples

- the naive negative {−q : q ∈ α} is not a cut
- why a cut is required to have no largest element (two cuts for the same rational otherwise)

## Exit checks (C-exercises)

- Prove the least upper bound property for cuts
- Prove that α + (−α) = 0* for an irrational cut and for a rational cut
