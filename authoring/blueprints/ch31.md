## Must prove

- uniqueness theorem (continuity point version)
- Σ|f̂(n)| < ∞ ⇒ S_N f → f uniformly (f continuous)
- f ∈ C² ⇒ f̂(n) = O(1/n²), hence uniform convergence
- (f*g)^(n) = f̂(n)ĝ(n)
- f*g is continuous for Riemann-integrable f, g
- approximation lemma: bounded Riemann-integrable f is the L¹ limit of continuous f_k with sup|f_k| ≤ sup|f|

## Key examples and counterexamples

- plucked string
- sawtooth f(x)=x on (−π,π)
- |x| on [−π,π] and Σ1/(2k+1)² = π²/8
- a continuous function whose Fourier series is not absolutely convergent (forward pointer)
