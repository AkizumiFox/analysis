## Must prove

- continuous functions on a closed rectangle are integrable
- discontinuity set of content zero ⇒ integrable
- Jordan-measurable sets form an algebra; integral over a Jordan set is well defined
- Fubini for continuous functions on rectangles and on type-I regions
- differentiation under the integral sign for continuous ∂f/∂t
- vol(T(A)) = |det T| vol(A) for linear T and Jordan A
- linear change-of-variables formula

## Key examples and counterexamples

- ℚ² ∩ [0,1]²: bounded, no content
- a compact set without content (fat Cantor square, GO.11)
- a function whose iterated integrals both exist and differ (GO.9)
- f(x,y) = (x²−y²)/(x²+y²)² on (0,1]²
- area of an ellipse by a linear change of variables

## Exit checks (C-exercises)

- prove Fubini for a continuous function on a rectangle
- prove vol(T(A)) = |det T| vol(A) for an elementary matrix, then in general
- use Fubini and a linear change of variables to compute the volume of an ellipsoid, justifying each step
