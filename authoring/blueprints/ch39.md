## Must prove

- ℛ[0,1] with the L² distance is not complete (explicit Cauchy sequence)
- a bounded monotone limit of Riemann-integrable functions need not be Riemann integrable
- the fat Cantor set is closed, nowhere dense, of positive outer Jordan content, and its indicator is not Riemann integrable
- the image of ℛ(𝕋) under f ↦ (f̂(n)) is a proper dense subspace of ℓ²

## Key examples and counterexamples

- indicator of the rationals as a monotone limit of indicators of finite sets
- fat Cantor set
- Volterra's function (construction sketched, key properties proved)
- Σ_{n} a_n e^{inx} with (a_n) ∈ ℓ² not the coefficients of any Riemann-integrable function
