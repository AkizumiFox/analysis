## Key examples and counterexamples

- polar coordinates: locally invertible everywhere off the origin, not globally injective
- x ↦ x³: bijective with non-invertible derivative at 0
- x ↦ x + x² sin(1/x): Df(0) invertible but f not injective near 0 (C¹ needed)
- x² + y² + z² = 1: tangent planes and a local graph
- maximize xyz on the sphere; min of a quadratic form on the sphere gives an eigenvector
